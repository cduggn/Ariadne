The mental model: an investigation is a growing conversation. Prefix is shared and cached, history is unique to the run, and each step pays for the whole history unless it lands on the worker that already holds it. That is why stickiness, the hop and the KV cap all exist. The ladder on the right is where every length limit in the system sits.
