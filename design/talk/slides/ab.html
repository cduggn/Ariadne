Why the gap is small here

At concurrency 4 load is low and even, so least-loaded lands on the right worker 77% of the time by luck. Both workers hold the 3.9k shared prefix from warm-up, so only each run's own history is at stake.

The 2-point pass difference is inside the 95% interval on 26 tasks. The policy is the only thing that changed between arms.

The lecturer asks why this policy over the alternatives. Answer: 94 percent of prompt tokens are the run's own history, so moving a run throws its cache away. The A/B measured that cost at ten percent more prefill and eight to eleven percent slower steps, with no change in answers. The gap is modest at low concurrency and we expect it to widen when load is uneven, which is exactly when least-loaded moves runs.
